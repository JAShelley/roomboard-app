    Keyboard shortcuts

    Run the board without a mouse. Shortcuts work when the board has focus and you're not typing in a field.

    Tab / Shift+Tab
    Move focus between room cards and controls.
    Shift
    With a room card focused, pick it up to move a patient. Press Shift again on another room to drop and swap.
    ← → ↑ ↓
    While carrying a patient, move to the destination room.
    Esc
    Cancel a move in progress, or close the checklist, notes, or Quick Add.
    Enter / Space
    Activate the focused control — discharge, ready toggles, or edit a checklist item.
    Shift+C
    Open or close the patient checklist.
