Security & Data Practices

Last updated July 3, 2026
No PHI stored by design TLS encryption in transit & at rest Database-level isolation per clinic

Overview

Most clinic software has to worry about protecting medical records. RoomBoard mostly doesn't — because we designed it so your board never needs your patients' protected health information (PHI) in the first place. That's the biggest security decision we made, and it's the one this page starts with.

Below is a plain-language summary of what RoomBoard stores, how it's isolated between clinics, and how the underlying infrastructure protects it. For our full data-handling terms, see the Privacy Policy. Questions we haven't answered here — including for larger or multi-location practices with specific security or procurement requirements — go to support@theroomboard.com.

What the board actually stores

A RoomBoard entry holds only what's needed to answer "who's where": a first name, a room, an appointment type, a provider, and timing. It does not hold diagnoses, chart notes, insurance details, or any other clinical record.

  • On the board: patient first name, room assignment, appointment type, assigned doctor/tech, and status timers.
  • Appointment capture: when you use the browser extension or desktop capture app to pull appointments from your scheduler, that data is read and matched on your device — only the fields above are ever sent to RoomBoard's servers.
  • Account data: staff names, emails, and clinic profile details you provide directly (practice name, specialty, phone, city/state).
  • Payment data: handled entirely by Stripe. RoomBoard's servers never see or store full card numbers.

How clinic data is isolated

RoomBoard is multi-tenant — every clinic shares the same application, but not the same data. Isolation is enforced at the database layer, not just in application code:

  • Every clinic's rooms, staff, boards, and settings are scoped to that clinic's account and enforced with Postgres row-level security policies, so a query from one clinic's session cannot return another clinic's rows even if the application code had a bug.
  • Staff join a clinic with an invite code generated by that clinic's admin — there's no way to land in the wrong clinic's board by accident.
  • Admin-level settings (billing, clinic profile, integrations) are restricted to clinic admins, not every team member.

Infrastructure & encryption

  • All traffic between your browser or the capture app and RoomBoard's servers is encrypted with TLS.
  • Data at rest is stored on our managed database provider's infrastructure, which encrypts data at rest by default.
  • Authentication (passwords, sessions) is handled by our managed auth provider rather than custom-built — passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text.

HIPAA & compliance posture

Because RoomBoard is built to run on first names, room numbers, and appointment types — not medical records — it's designed to minimize the PHI exposure a typical clinic tool would carry. That said, we want to be direct about where we are today rather than overstate it: we do not currently hold a SOC 2 report or a signed Business Associate Agreement (BAA) program. If your practice's compliance or procurement process needs to evaluate that in detail, email support@theroomboard.com and we'll work through your specific requirements with you.

Reporting a security issue

If you believe you've found a security vulnerability in RoomBoard, please email support@theroomboard.com with details. We ask that you give us a reasonable window to investigate and fix an issue before disclosing it publicly.
