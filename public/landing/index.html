Questions, answered

Any practice that moves patients through rooms — medical (MD), osteopathic (DO), dental, physical therapy, veterinary, and more. You define your own rooms, providers, and visit types, so it fits how your office actually runs.

Every device for your clinic shares one live board. A change on the front desk shows up on the wall display and on the phone in the back in under a second, and a sync indicator tells you the board is current.

Instead of retyping a patient, you click the appointment in your existing scheduler. The native helper reads it (with on-device OCR as a backup for image-based schedulers), pre-fills a quick-send card, and you confirm the room. A browser extension covers web schedulers too.
