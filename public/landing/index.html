What the timers are for

RoomBoard is a customer-service tool. The timer exists so someone can step in and check on a patient who's been waiting — offer an update, a glass of water, a realistic ETA — before a long wait turns into a bad visit. It's not a stopwatch on your providers; if it also helps you see where the day is getting backed up, that's a welcome bonus — but the patient in the chair always comes first.

Questions, answered

Any practice that moves patients through rooms — medical (MD), osteopathic (DO), dental, physical therapy, veterinary, and more. You define your own rooms, providers, and visit types, so it fits how your office actually runs.

Every device for your clinic shares one live board. A change on the front desk shows up on the wall display and on the phone in the back in under a second, and a sync indicator tells you the board is current.

Instead of retyping a patient, you click the appointment in your existing scheduler. The native helper reads it (with on-device OCR as a backup for image-based schedulers), pre-fills a quick-send card, and you confirm the room. A browser extension covers web schedulers too.

No — open the board in any browser to start. Install the desktop apps when you want a dedicated wall display, and add the capture helper when you're ready to pull appointments in automatically.

RoomBoard is designed so you never have to send protected health information to us. The board holds first names, room assignments, and appointment types — not medical records or clinical notes. Appointment capture keeps everything on your device until you choose to send it. Each clinic gets its own isolated account with encrypted connections, and no clinic can see another's data.
