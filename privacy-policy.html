We keep your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Notice, unless a longer retention period is required by law. We retain information for as long as you have an account with us.

If we learn that personal information from users under 18 has been collected, we will deactivate the account and delete such data. If you become aware of this, please contact us at roomboardsupport@gmail.com.

7. Do-Not-Track Features

Most web browsers and mobile operating systems include a Do-Not-Track (DNT) feature. At this time, no uniform technology standard for recognizing DNT signals has been finalized. As such, we do not currently respond to DNT browser signals or other mechanisms that automatically communicate your preference not to be tracked.
