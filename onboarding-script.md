# RoomBoard — Onboarding Video Script

**Audience:** A new clinic admin (and their staff) setting up RoomBoard for the first time.
**Length target:** 3–4 minutes.
**Tone:** Calm, practical, "let's get you running in 5 minutes" — not salesy. They already bought in; this is the getting-started.
**Format notes:** Each scene lists the **on-screen action** (what the recording shows), the **caption** (short text overlay for sound-off viewers), and the **VO** (voiceover narration). Every screen/label below matches the real app.

---

## Scene 0 — Cold open (0:00–0:12)

- **On screen:** The live Display board on a wall TV — room cards filling a clean grid, a couple of timers ticking, a doctor badge or two. Subtle motion only.
- **Caption:** `This is your whole clinic, at a glance.`
- **VO:** "This is RoomBoard. By the end of this video, your clinic will have a live room board that everyone — front desk, techs, doctors — can read at a glance. Let's set it up. It takes about five minutes."

---

## Scene 1 — Create or join your clinic (0:12–0:45)

- **On screen:** The sign-in screen, "Sign in to your clinic." Cursor hovers the two paths: **Create clinic** and **Join existing clinic**.
- **Caption:** `First admin → Create clinic. Everyone else → Join.`
- **VO:** "First time here, you'll see two choices. If you're the first person at your practice to set this up, pick **Create clinic** — give it a name, like your practice name, and you become the admin. Everyone else on your team will pick **Join existing clinic** instead."

- **On screen (Create path):** Type clinic name into **Clinic name** field → land on **Configure your clinic**. Then open the clinic settings and highlight the **Clinic invite code** with the **Copy code** button.
- **Caption:** `Share the invite code with your team.`
- **VO:** "Once your clinic exists, grab your **invite code** from clinic settings and share it with your team. They'll enter that code on the **Join clinic** screen — no separate accounts to manage, and everyone lands on the same board."

> **Recording tip:** Use a throwaway demo clinic name (e.g. "Demo Animal Hospital") so nothing real is on screen.

---

## Scene 2 — Add your rooms (0:45–1:25)

- **On screen:** The Display view. Cursor clicks **Add room** (the `+` / "Add or edit a room from Display"). Type into **New room name** — placeholder shows `e.g., Room 10`. Add a few: "Room 1," "Room 2," "Dental," "Surgery." Cards appear in the grid as you go.
- **Caption:** `Add a card for every room you want to track.`
- **VO:** "Now the core of it — your rooms. Click **Add room**, type a name, and a card appears on the board. Add one for every space you want to track: exam rooms, dental, surgery, the cleaning bay — whatever matters in your clinic. You can rename or reorder these any time, so don't overthink it."

---

## Scene 3 — Add your doctors and techs (1:25–2:00)

- **On screen:** Open the **Doctors** section in settings. Add a doctor name; show the **Doctor initials** / **Doctor badge** preview updating. Optionally show **Highlight doctor**.
- **Caption:** `Add your team once — reuse them all day.`
- **VO:** "Add your doctors and techs once, and they become quick-pick badges you can drop onto any room. Each doctor gets a colored badge with their initials, so a glance at the board tells you who's where. You can highlight a specific doctor when you need to follow their flow."

---

## Scene 4 — The daily workflow: Quick Add (2:00–2:45)

- **On screen:** Click **Quick Add** to open the modal. Fill it in: **Patient name**, **Type** (appointment type), pick a **Doctor** and **Tech**, maybe a **Status note**. Hit **Add** → the room card updates instantly with patient, doctor badge, tech, and a running timer.
- **Caption:** `Quick Add = one place to update a room.`
- **VO:** "Here's the move you'll make a hundred times a day: **Quick Add**. Open it, drop in the patient, the appointment type, the doctor and tech — and the room card updates instantly, with the timer already running. This is how a room goes from empty to occupied in a couple of seconds. No digging through menus."

> **Recording tip:** Use a fake patient name ("Bella," "Max") — never a real client/patient.

---

## Scene 5 — Mark ready & cleaning timers (2:45–3:20)

- **On screen:** On a room card, flip the **ready** toggle → a **ready badge** appears. Then end the visit and start a **cleaning** timer; show the cleaning timer ticking, then clearing when done.
- **Caption:** `Ready badges + cleaning timers keep rooms moving.`
- **VO:** "When a room's prepped, flip it to **ready** — a badge shows the whole team it's good to go. When a patient leaves, the **cleaning timer** starts, so nobody walks a client into a room that isn't turned over yet. The board does the remembering for you."

---

## Scene 6 — The Display view for your screens (3:20–3:45)

- **On screen:** Click **Open board** / fullscreen the **Display view** on a wall TV. Show the big, readable grid. Briefly show **Card layout** / **Card scale** / **Display colors** being nudged so it reads from across the room.
- **Caption:** `Put it on a screen everyone can see.`
- **VO:** "Cast this to a TV in the treatment area and you've got a single source of truth for the whole team. Tweak the card size, layout, and colors so it reads from across the room. Everyone updating from their own device, one board everyone sees."

---

## Scene 7 — Close (3:45–4:00)

- **On screen:** Back to the full live board, calm and busy. RoomBoard logo.
- **Caption:** `That's it — you're live.`
- **VO:** "That's the whole thing. Add your team, share the invite code, and let the board run your floor. Questions? There's a feedback button right in the app — we read every one. Welcome to RoomBoard."

---

## Production checklist

- [ ] Use a demo clinic + fake patient/doctor names throughout — no real PHI/client data on screen.
- [ ] Record at the resolution the wall TVs use (looks best at the Display view's intended size).
- [ ] Keep cursor movements slow and deliberate; pause ~1s after each click before the next.
- [ ] Captions on by default — most clinic staff will watch sound-off.
- [ ] If animated instead of screencast: I can capture real screenshots of each scene from the live app and sequence them with these captions + an AI voiceover.

## Open questions for you

1. **Admin vs. all-staff:** Should this be one video, or split into a short "admin setup" (Scenes 1–3) and a shorter "daily use" (Scenes 4–6) for staff? Daily-use staff don't need the create-clinic part.
2. **Invite code on screen:** OK to show a fake code, or keep it fully off-screen?
3. **Length:** Is ~4 min right, or do you want a tight 90-second version?
